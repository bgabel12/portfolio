<p>
  At the time, the existing third-party solution for machine data collection was costly, outdated, and inefficient.
  Customers faced long wait times for implementation due to the vendor’s limited installation team,
  delaying access to critical operational data. The reliance on proprietary hardware and software
  further increased costs and restricted flexibility.
</p>

<p>
  To address these challenges, I developed <strong>Alora Edge</strong>, a robust and scalable
  <strong>edge service</strong> designed to collect, standardize, and store machine data using
  widely adopted industrial protocols. This solution enabled manufacturers to streamline
  data acquisition, reduce costs, and eliminate long lead times associated with third-party installations.
</p>

<h5 class="mt-4">Key Features</h5>
<ul class="list-group list-group-flush">
  <li class="list-group-item">
    <strong>Windows Service for Data Collection:</strong> Built using
    <strong>C# and .NET Core</strong>, the service connects to machines and devices
    through <strong>MTConnect, OPC UA, MQTT</strong>, and other protocols,
    ensuring compatibility with a wide range of industrial equipment.
  </li>
  <li class="list-group-item">
    <strong>Blazor-Based Management Interface:</strong> Developed a
    <strong>Blazor application</strong> to provide an intuitive interface
    for configuring machine connectivity, managing data collection settings,
    and selecting relevant data points. A <strong>SQLite database</strong>
    was used to store configurations for both the service and the management application.
  </li>
  <li class="list-group-item">
    <strong>Custom Installer for Deployment:</strong> Created a
    <strong>WiX Toolset-based installer</strong>, simplifying the deployment process
    for customers and technical support teams. This eliminated the need for manual setup,
    reducing installation complexity and ensuring a seamless experience.
  </li>
</ul>

<h5 class="mt-4">Impact</h5>
<p>
  By implementing <strong>Alora Edge</strong>, manufacturers gained a
  <strong>cost-effective, flexible, and self-managed</strong> data collection system.
  The solution reduced reliance on third-party vendors, allowing for
  <strong>faster deployment, improved data accessibility, and long-term cost savings</strong>.
  The use of <strong>widely supported industrial protocols</strong> ensured seamless integration
  with existing infrastructure, enabling organizations to make real-time, data-driven
  decisions without being locked into proprietary ecosystems.
</p>
